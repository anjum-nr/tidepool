From lump of clay to finished bowl

A piece goes through six stages before you take it home. You do the first three with us, and we handle the kiln work.

  1. Wedge

    Knead the clay to push out air bubbles, which can crack a pot in the kiln.

  2. Throw

    Center the clay on the wheel, open it up and pull the walls to height.

  3. Trim

    Once the pot is leather-hard, shave the base and carve a neat foot ring.

  4. Bisque fire

    A first firing to about 1000°C turns soft clay into porous, glazeable ceramic.

  5. Glaze

    Dip or brush on glaze. It looks chalky now and changes color in the kiln.

  6. Glaze fire

    A cone 6 firing, around 1220°C, melts the glaze into a hard, food-safe surface.
